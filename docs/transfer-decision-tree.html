PikaShot transfers: every case, from Send to resolved in Check

How the app works today (v113), step by step: what the sender does, what the receiving store owner sees, what happens to each item depending on its unit and price, and how each job is finished in the Check tab. Diamonds are decisions; rounded boxes are where a path starts or ends.

1. Sending a transfer (store owner or their salesperson)

A transfer is always made from sales already recorded. Items are grouped per product; each carries its name, barcode, unit, quantity, the sender's price, any discount, and credit paid/owed.

2. Receiving and accepting

Only store owners receive transfers. Salespeople never see them, and there are no transfer QR codes any more. Busy? = a box is open or the scanner is on (it waits, up to 5 minutes, then shows anyway).

3. What happens to each item (units)

"Cost" here is what one cost in this transfer: the sender's price, less this line's share of any discount. "Bulk" units are Packs and Cartons; "smaller" units are Sachets, Bottles, Cans, Packets (and older Tins, Pieces). One of your products? = the same barcode, or the carton/pack barcode remembered on it, or the same name when one of them has no barcode. Answered before? = you once kept your own unit when this other unit came. Needs you for anything? = something to count, a unit question, a price to set, or a price at or below the cost.

4. Price check (every item, after chart 3)

The selling price is never changed by a transfer. Only the cost moves (and only in the cases marked in chart 3).

6. Resolving each job in Check

An item stays in Check until nothing is left for it. All always lists every product; Check is only the list of jobs. Each card talks (Pidgin) and has a speaker to mute it.
